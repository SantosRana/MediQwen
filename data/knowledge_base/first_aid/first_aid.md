# First Aid Emergencies

## General Guidance
- Check safety for yourself and the injured person.  
- Call **999** (UK emergency services) when needed.  
- Carry out basic first aid until help arrives.  

## Unconscious Person
- **Breathing:** Place in recovery position, monitor breathing.  
- **Not breathing:** Call 999, start CPR immediately.  

## Common Emergencies

### Anaphylaxis
- Severe allergic reaction (food, insect sting).  
- Symptoms: airway swelling, breathing difficulty.  
- Action: Call 999, use adrenaline auto-injector if available, keep person comfortable.  
- All cases require medical follow-up.  

### Heavy Bleeding
- Call 999.  
- Apply firm pressure with clean pad/dressing.  
- Do not remove embedded objects; pad around them.  
- If limb severed: wrap, place in plastic bag, keep cool (not directly on ice).  

### Burns & Scalds
- Cool under running water ≥20 minutes.  
- Remove clothing/jewellery unless stuck.  
- Cover loosely with cling film or clean dressing.  
- Do not apply creams/oils.  
- Watch for hypothermia in children/elderly.  

### Chemical Burns
- Wear gloves, remove contaminated clothing.  
- Rinse with cool water ≥20 minutes.  
- Call 999.  

### Choking
- **Mild:** Encourage coughing.  
- **Severe:** 5 back blows → 5 abdominal thrusts (not for infants <1 or pregnant women).  
- Continue cycles until obstruction clears or help arrives.  
- Always seek medical check after choking.  

### Drowning
- Do not enter water unless safe.  
- Once on land: check breathing.  
- If not breathing: 5 rescue breaths → CPR.  
- If breathing: recovery position, call ambulance.  

### Electric Shock
- Switch off power at mains before touching victim.  
- If not breathing: call 999, start CPR.  
- Seek medical help after any shock.  

### Fractures
- Treat as broken bone if unsure.  
- Keep still, call 999 if severe pain, leg/back injury.  
- Do not give food/drink (possible anaesthetic needed).  

### Heart Attack
- Symptoms: chest pain (pressure/tightness), pain radiating to arms/jaw/back.  
- Call 999, sit person comfortably.  
- Give 300mg aspirin to chew (unless contraindicated).  
- Monitor breathing; start CPR if unconscious.  

### Needlestick Injuries
- Encourage bleeding, wash with soap/water.  
- Cover with waterproof dressing.  
- Seek urgent medical advice (NHS 111 or A&E).  

### Poisoning
- Causes: toxic substances, overdose, alcohol.  
- Call 999 immediately.  
- Do not give food/drink or induce vomiting.  
- Collect vomit sample if occurs.  
- If unconscious: recovery position, monitor breathing, CPR if needed.  

### Shock
- Signs: pale clammy skin, rapid breathing, dizziness, nausea.  
- Call 999.  
- Lay person down, raise legs if possible.  
- Keep warm, do not give food/drink.  
- Monitor, start CPR if breathing stops.  

### Stroke
- Use **FAST** test:  
  - Face drooping  
  - Arm weakness  
  - Speech difficulty  
  - Time to call 999 immediately  
- Even if symptoms resolve (possible TIA), seek urgent medical help.  

## Notes
- First aid saves lives before emergency services arrive.  
- Always seek professional medical care after serious incidents.
