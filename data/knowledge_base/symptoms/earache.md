# Earache

## Overview
- Earache and ear pain are common, especially in children.
- Usually not serious and often improve within a few days.
- Can affect one or both ears.

## Duration
- Most earaches in children are caused by ear infections.
- Symptoms usually start to improve after a few days.

## Symptoms in Babies and Young Children
Signs may include:
- Rubbing or pulling at the ear
- Not reacting to some sounds
- Temperature ≥38°C
- Irritability or restlessness
- Loss of appetite
- Balance problems

## Self Care
**Do:**
- Use painkillers (paracetamol or ibuprofen; no aspirin for under 16s)
- Place a warm flannel on the ear

**Don’t:**
- Do not put anything inside the ear (cotton buds, etc.)
- Do not try to remove earwax
- Do not let water enter the ear

**Note:** Some painkillers are not safe for everyone (e.g., pregnancy). Always check leaflet or seek advice.

## Pharmacist Help
Pharmacists can advise on:
- Self-care measures
- Over-the-counter treatments (e.g., eardrops)
- Whether GP consultation is needed
- Treating middle ear infections in children aged 1–17

## When to See a GP
See a GP if:
- You or your child keep getting earache

**Urgent GP / NHS 111:**
Seek urgent help if earache persists >2–3 days or if:
- General unwellness
- Very high temperature, feeling hot/cold/shivery
- Swelling around the ear
- Fluid discharge from the ear
- Hearing loss or changes
- Object stuck in the ear
- Child <2 years with earache in both ears

**Call 111 for advice** if child <5 years old has earache.

## Causes
Earache is often due to infection, but many causes exist. Examples:

| Symptom Pattern | Possible Cause |
|-----------------|----------------|
| Ear pain with toothache | Teething, dental abscess |
| Ear pain + hearing changes | Glue ear, earwax build-up, object stuck, perforated eardrum |
| Ear pain + pain when swallowing | Sore throat, tonsillitis, quinsy |
| Ear pain + fever | Ear infection, flu, cold |

## Notes
- Most earaches resolve naturally.
- Persistent or severe cases require medical evaluation.
- Do not attempt self-diagnosis or removal of objects/earwax.
