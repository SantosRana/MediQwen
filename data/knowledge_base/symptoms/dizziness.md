# Dizziness

## Overview
- Feeling dizzy, lightheaded, or off-balance is common.
- Usually not serious and often resolves on its own.
- Can include vertigo (spinning sensation).

## Symptoms
Dizziness may feel like:
- Off-balance
- Giddy
- Lightheaded or faint
- Spinning or surroundings spinning (vertigo)

## Self Care
**Do:**
- Lie down until dizziness passes, then get up slowly
- Move slowly and carefully
- Get plenty of rest
- Drink fluids, especially water
- Avoid coffee, cigarettes, alcohol, and recreational drugs

**Don’t:**
- Do not bend down suddenly
- Do not get up suddenly after sitting or lying down
- Avoid dangerous activities while dizzy (driving, climbing ladders, using heavy machinery)

## When to See a GP
Seek medical advice if:
- Dizziness or vertigo persists or keeps coming back
- You’re worried about symptoms
- Difficulty hearing or speaking
- Ringing in ears (tinnitus)
- Double or blurred vision, or other eyesight changes
- Numbness or weakness in face, arms, or legs
- Other symptoms: irregular pulse, fainting, collapsing, headaches, nausea/vomiting

## Causes
- Often linked to other illnesses or temporary conditions.
- Can occur without obvious reason.
- Underlying causes vary; do not self-diagnose.

## Notes
- Most dizziness is mild and self-limiting.
- Persistent or severe dizziness requires medical evaluation.
- Vertigo is a specific type of dizziness involving spinning sensations.
