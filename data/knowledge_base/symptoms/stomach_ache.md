# Stomach Ache

## Overview
- Most stomach aches are not serious.
- Often resolve within a few days.
- Causes range from minor digestive issues to more serious conditions.

## Common Causes
| Type of Stomach Ache | Possible Condition |
|----------------------|--------------------|
| Feeling bloated, farting a lot | Trapped wind (flatulence) |
| Feeling full/bloated after eating, heartburn, nausea | Indigestion |
| Cannot poo | Constipation |
| Watery poo, nausea, vomiting | Stomach bug or food poisoning |

## Pharmacist Help
Pharmacists can advise on:
- Possible causes
- Treatments that may help
- Medicines for constipation and indigestion

## When to Seek Medical Help
**Urgent GP / NHS 111:**
Seek help if stomach ache:
- Gets much worse
- Does not go away or keeps coming back
- Persistent bloating
- Difficulty swallowing food
- Occurs during pregnancy
- Unexplained weight loss
- Sudden changes in urination (frequency or pain)
- Bleeding from bottom
- Unusual vaginal bleeding/discharge
- Diarrhoea lasting > few days

**Emergency (999 / A&E):**
Call immediately if:
- Sudden or severe stomach ache
- Pain when touching stomach
- Vomiting blood or vomit looks like ground coffee
- Bloody or black, sticky, foul-smelling poo
- Unable to pee, poo, or fart
- Breathing difficulty
- Chest pain
- Vomiting with diabetes
- Collapse

**Note:** Do not drive to A&E. Bring current medicines with you.

## Less Common Causes
| Type of Stomach Ache | Possible Condition |
|----------------------|--------------------|
| Pain/cramps during period | Period pain |
| Sudden pain in lower right side | Appendicitis |
| Ongoing cramps, bloating, diarrhoea, constipation | Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) |
| Severe pain radiating to groin, nausea, painful urination | Kidney stones |
| Severe pain lasting hours in centre or under right ribs | Gallstones |

## Notes
- Do not self-diagnose.
- Most stomach aches are mild, but persistent or severe cases require medical evaluation.
- Causes range from digestive issues to serious conditions like appendicitis or gallstones.
