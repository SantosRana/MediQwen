# Bronchitis

## Overview
- Bronchitis is inflammation of the airways in the lungs, usually caused by infection.
- Often clears up without treatment in ~3 weeks.
- Chronic bronchitis is long-term inflammation and part of COPD.

## Symptoms
- Cough (clear, white, yellow, or green mucus)
- Chest pain when coughing
- Shortness of breath
- Sore throat
- Runny nose
- High temperature
- Symptoms similar to cold or flu

## Self Care
**Do:**
- Rest, avoid contact with others if feverish
- Drink plenty of fluids
- Take painkillers (paracetamol, ibuprofen)
- Honey in warm drinks (not for babies <1 year)
- Cover mouth/nose when coughing/sneezing
- Dispose of tissues promptly
- Wash hands regularly

**Don’t:**
- Do not smoke

## When to Seek Help
- **Urgent GP/111:**  
  - Cough >3 weeks  
  - Coughing blood/blood-stained mucus  
  - Chest pain (on/off, or when breathing/coughing)  
  - Age >65, pregnancy, long-term conditions (diabetes, heart/lung/kidney disease)  
  - Weakened immune system (e.g., chemotherapy)  
  - Feeling too unwell for daily activities  
  - Shortness of breath  

- **999 / A&E:**  
  - Struggling to breathe (choking, gasping, unable to speak)  
  - Pale/blue/blotchy skin, lips, tongue (check gums/nails/eyes in darker skin)  
  - Sudden confusion  
  - Baby unresponsive/floppy  

## Treatments
- Usually clears without treatment in ~3 weeks.
- GP if symptoms persist >3 weeks.
- Antibiotics may be needed if bacterial infection suspected.

## Notes
- Bronchitis is common and usually mild.
- Chronic bronchitis is part of COPD and requires long-term management.
- Prevent spread by good hygiene and avoiding smoking.
