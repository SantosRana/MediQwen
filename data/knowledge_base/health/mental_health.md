# Mental Health

## Key Facts
- Over **1 billion people** live with a mental health condition globally.  
- Affordable, effective strategies exist to promote, protect, and restore mental health.  
- Mental health is integral to well-being and is a basic human right.  
- Urgent action is needed to close treatment gaps worldwide.  

## Overview
- Mental health is a state of well-being enabling people to cope with stress, realize abilities, learn, work, and contribute to community.  
- Exists on a continuum, shaped by individual, family, community, and structural factors.  
- Conditions include mental disorders, psychosocial disabilities, and states of distress or risk of self-harm.  
- Many conditions can be treated at low cost, but health systems remain under-resourced.  

## Risks & Protective Factors
- **Risks:**  
  - Individual: emotional skills, substance use, genetics.  
  - Social/environmental: poverty, violence, inequality, deprivation.  
  - Sensitive periods: early childhood (harsh parenting, bullying).  
- **Protective factors:**  
  - Emotional/social skills, positive interactions, education, decent work, safe communities.  
- Global threats: economic downturns, disease outbreaks, humanitarian crises, displacement, climate change.  
- Outcomes shaped by interplay of determinants; not all exposed develop conditions.  

## Promotion & Prevention
- Aim: improve mental health by addressing determinants.  
- Requires cross-sector collaboration (education, labour, justice, housing, environment).  
- **Suicide prevention:** limit access to means, responsible media, adolescent social-emotional learning, early intervention, banning hazardous pesticides.  
- **Child/adolescent mental health:** caregiver support, school-based programmes, safe online/community environments.  
- **Workplace mental health:** legislation, policies, manager training, targeted interventions.  

## Care & Treatment
- Focus on both promotion and addressing needs of those with conditions.  
- **Community-based care:** accessible, acceptable, prevents rights violations, improves recovery.  
- Coordinated services:  
  - Integrated mental health in general health care (hospitals, primary care).  
  - Dedicated community services (centres, teams, peer support, supported living).  
  - Support in non-health settings (schools, child protection, prisons).  
- Innovative approaches: non-specialist psychological interventions, digital self-help tools.  
- Vast care gap for depression, anxiety, and other common conditions. 

## 5 Steps to Mental Wellbeing

Evidence suggests there are 5 steps you can take to improve your mental health and wellbeing. Trying these things could help you feel more positive and able to get the most out of life.

---

## 1. Connect with Other People
- Good relationships are important for mental wellbeing:
  - Build a sense of belonging and self-worth
  - Share positive experiences
  - Provide and receive emotional support

**Do:**
- Spend time daily with family (e.g., dinner together)
- Arrange outings with friends
- Switch off the TV to talk or play games
- Have lunch with a colleague
- Visit someone who needs company
- Volunteer at a local school, hospital, or community group
- Use video-chat apps to stay in touch

**Don’t:**
- Rely only on technology or social media to build relationships

---

## 2. Be Physically Active
- Benefits of activity:
  - Improves physical health and fitness
  - Raises self-esteem
  - Helps set and achieve goals
  - Causes positive chemical changes in the brain

**Do:**
- Try running or aerobic exercises
- Improve strength and flexibility
- Follow fitness advice for wheelchair users

**Don’t:**
- Feel pressured to spend hours in the gym — choose activities you enjoy

---

## 3. Learn New Skills
- Benefits of learning:
  - Boosts self-confidence and self-esteem
  - Builds a sense of purpose
  - Helps connect with others

**Do:**
- Learn to cook new dishes
- Take on new responsibilities at work
- Work on DIY projects
- Sign up for a local course (language, practical skills)
- Try hobbies that challenge you (blogging, sports, painting)

**Don’t:**
- Feel you must pursue qualifications or exams if uninterested

---

## 4. Give to Others
- Acts of kindness improve wellbeing:
  - Create positive feelings and rewards
  - Provide purpose and self-worth
  - Strengthen connections with others

**Examples:**
- Say thank you
- Ask and listen to how others are doing
- Spend time with those needing support
- Offer help with projects
- Volunteer in your community

---

## 5. Pay Attention to the Present Moment (Mindfulness)
- Mindfulness involves awareness of:
  - Thoughts and feelings
  - Body
  - Surroundings

- Benefits:
  - Enjoy life more
  - Understand yourself better
  - Positively change how you approach challenges

**Do:**
- Practice mindfulness daily
- Explore steps to be more mindful in everyday life


## Notes
- Mental health has intrinsic and instrumental value.  
- Strengthening systems, reducing stigma, and scaling affordable interventions are global priorities.
