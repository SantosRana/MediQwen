# COVID-19 (SARS-CoV-2)

## Overview
- Caused by SARS-CoV-2 virus, spreads via respiratory particles and contaminated surfaces.
- Global pandemic since 2019, continues to circulate worldwide.
- Most people recover without treatment due to immunity from infection or vaccination.
- About 6% develop **post COVID-19 condition (PCC/long COVID)** with lasting symptoms.
- Older adults, unvaccinated individuals, and those with underlying conditions are at higher risk of severe illness.
- Vaccination remains the most effective preventive measure against severe disease and death.

## Symptoms
- **Common:** fever, chills, sore throat.  
- **Less common:** muscle aches, fatigue, runny nose, headache, sore eyes, dizziness, cough, chest pain, shortness of breath, hoarse voice, numbness, appetite loss, nausea, diarrhoea, loss/change of taste or smell, sleep difficulties.  
- **Severe (seek immediate care):** difficulty breathing, confusion, drowsiness, chest pain, cold/clammy skin, bluish skin/lips, loss of speech or movement.  
- Children may rarely develop severe inflammatory syndrome weeks after infection.

## Transmission
- Spread via respiratory particles when infected person breathes, talks, coughs, sneezes.
- Higher risk in close contact or poorly ventilated indoor spaces.
- Can spread via contaminated surfaces (hands → eyes/nose/mouth).
- Symptoms usually begin 3–6 days after exposure, last up to 10 days.
- Asymptomatic and pre-symptomatic transmission possible.

## Treatment
- Most mild cases recover without treatment.
- Severe cases: individualized care based on age, comorbidities, and risk factors.
- Treatments may include antivirals, corticosteroids, oxygen therapy, or advanced supportive care.
- PCC (long COVID) requires tailored management.

## Prevention
- Stay home if symptomatic or positive.
- Avoid close contact with high-risk individuals.
- Wear a properly fitted mask if symptomatic or in crowded/poorly ventilated areas.
- Practice respiratory hygiene (cover mouth/nose when coughing/sneezing).
- Wash hands frequently with soap or alcohol rub.
- Improve indoor ventilation.
- Health workers: use medical masks, PPE for aerosol-generating procedures.

## Vaccination
- Vaccines save millions of lives by preventing severe disease, hospitalization, and death.
- WHO (Nov 2023) guidance:  
  - Single dose for unvaccinated individuals.  
  - Revaccination every 6–12 months for high-priority groups (older adults, comorbidities, immunocompromised, pregnant women, health workers).  
- Vaccines protect against severe illness but have limited effect on transmission.
- WHO SAGE provides global vaccination recommendations; follow local health authority guidance.

## Complications
- Severe COVID-19 may cause respiratory failure, sepsis, thromboembolism, multiorgan failure, and death.
- Long COVID (PCC): fatigue, breathlessness, headaches, cognitive difficulties, joint/muscle pain.
- Rare: Ramsay Hunt syndrome, inflammatory syndromes in children.

## Notes
- COVID-19 remains a global health threat with ongoing outbreaks.
- Vaccination and preventive measures are critical to reducing severe outcomes.
