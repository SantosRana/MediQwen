# Heart Attack (Myocardial Infarction)

## Overview
- A heart attack occurs when blood flow to the heart is blocked.
- Life-threatening emergency requiring immediate hospital treatment.
- Can cause permanent damage or death if untreated.

## Symptoms
- Chest pain (crushing, squeezing, tightness) spreading to arm, neck, jaw
- Shortness of breath
- Nausea or vomiting
- Indigestion-like burning, bloating
- Sweating
- Pale, blue, or grey skin (on darker skin, check palms)

## Emergency (999 / A&E)
Call immediately if:
- Chest pain feels tight/squeezing
- Pain spreads to arms, neck, jaw
- Severe breathing difficulty
- Pale/blue/grey lips or skin
- Someone has collapsed and is unresponsive

**Note:** Do not drive yourself. Ambulance transport required.

## NHS 111
Call if:
- Sudden worsening breathlessness
- Chest pain with nausea/vomiting
- Chest pain with irregular heartbeat or fluttering

## While Waiting for Help
- Sit and rest comfortably (knees bent, back supported)
- Take aspirin (300mg) if available and no allergy
- Use GTN spray if prescribed for angina
- Monitor responsiveness and breathing
- Start CPR if person stops breathing

## Diagnosis
- ECG and blood tests confirm heart attack
- Further tests: echocardiogram (damage), coronary angiogram (blockage location)

## Treatments
- **Hospital care:** medicines, angioplasty (stent), coronary artery bypass surgery
- **Medicines after heart attack:**
  - Antiplatelets (prevent clots)
  - Statins (lower cholesterol)
  - ACE inhibitors, beta-blockers (control blood pressure, protect heart)

## Recovery
- Varies: weeks to months
- Rest and time off work
- Cardiac rehabilitation (in-person or remote)
- Most people resume normal activities
- Emotional support may be needed for anxiety/low mood

## Causes
- Usually caused by blood clot blocking coronary artery
- Risk factors:
  - Smoking
  - High blood pressure
  - High cholesterol
  - Family history
  - Diabetes
  - Overweight/obesity

## Prevention
**Do:**
- Stop smoking
- Maintain healthy cholesterol and blood pressure
- Eat balanced diet (low salt, low saturated fat)
- Maintain healthy weight
- Exercise regularly
- Limit alcohol

## Support
- Emotional support groups (local or online)
- British Heart Foundation helpline: 080 802 1234

## Notes
- Heart attack ≠ cardiac arrest (cardiac arrest = heart stops suddenly).
- Immediate treatment saves lives and reduces complications.
- Long-term medicines and lifestyle changes reduce recurrence risk.
