# Coronary Heart Disease (CHD)

## Overview
- CHD is the leading cause of death worldwide.
- Occurs when coronary arteries narrow due to fatty deposits (atheroma).
- Can lead to angina, heart attacks, and heart failure.

## Symptoms
- Chest pain (angina), often triggered by activity or stress
- Shortness of breath
- Heart palpitations
- Dizziness or fainting
- Heart attack symptoms: severe chest pain, sweating, nausea, pain spreading to arms/jaw/back

## Causes
- Build-up of fatty deposits in coronary arteries (atherosclerosis)
- Risk factors include:
  - Smoking
  - High blood pressure
  - High cholesterol
  - Diabetes
  - Obesity
  - Sedentary lifestyle
  - Family history of heart disease

## Prevention
- Stop smoking
- Eat a balanced, low-fat diet
- Exercise regularly
- Maintain healthy weight
- Limit alcohol
- Manage blood pressure, cholesterol, and diabetes

## Treatments
- **Lifestyle changes:** diet, exercise, smoking cessation
- **Medicines:** statins, aspirin, beta-blockers, ACE inhibitors
- **Procedures:** angioplasty, stent insertion, coronary artery bypass surgery

## When to Seek Help
- Call 999 if chest pain is severe, sudden, or accompanied by breathlessness, sweating, or nausea (possible heart attack).
- See GP if experiencing recurring chest pain or risk factors.

## Notes
- CHD is chronic but manageable with lifestyle and medical treatment.
- Early diagnosis and prevention significantly reduce risk of complications.
